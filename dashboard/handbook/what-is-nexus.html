What is Nexus Memory?

Nexus Memory is a long-term memory for your AI agents. Think of it like this: your AI agents can chat fine, but everything they "know" evaporates when the conversation ends. Nexus Memory gives them a shared, permanent brain they can write to and read from, one brain for all your agents, so what one agent learns, every other agent can use.

What it does in one sentence

When your agent learns something worth keeping, it stores it in Nexus Memory. Next time, even weeks later, even a different agent, the knowledge is back on the table automatically, before your agent even answers.

Where it lives

100% on your own machine. Nothing is sent to us. The two pieces are:

  • Qdrant, the archive itself. A small database whose whole job is to store memories and find them by meaning, not just by keywords. It runs as a local service on your computer.
  • The Nexus service, the librarian in front of the archive. It receives "remember this" and "what do we know about X" requests from your agents, sorts, connects and protects everything.

How "understanding" works (embeddings, no jargon)

Every memory is converted into a long list of numbers, think of it as a fingerprint of the meaning. "My dog is called Buddy" and "Buddy is the family dog" produce almost identical fingerprints, even though the words differ. When an agent asks a question, Nexus fingerprints the question and finds memories with the closest fingerprints. That is why you can ask for "the dog's birthday" and still find a memory that only says "Buddy, born in March".

Why it feels like the agent "remembers"

Most memory tools only answer when explicitly asked. Nexus also pushes: before every conversation turn, relevant memories are fetched automatically and laid on the table. You don't have to remember what to ask for, that's the point.

Good to know: Deleting a chat history never deletes the memory store. And the reverse: memories persist even if you re-install an agent. The brain outlives the agents, "agents come and go, Nexus Memory stays."

What it is not

  • It is not a chat program, your agents chat elsewhere; Nexus is their memory.
  • It is not a cloud service. No account, no upload, no telemetry. If it can't reach the internet, it still works.
  • It is not magic: it only knows what was stored. Garbage stored → garbage found. That's what SICA (cleaning) and Guardrails (protection) are for, see The building blocks.
