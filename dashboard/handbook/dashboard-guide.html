Dashboard guide

The top bar

ElementWhat it tells you
Version badge (top right)your installed Nexus Memory version.
Health dot (green/yellow/red)at a glance: is everything reachable and healthy? Click ❤️ Health below for details.

The stat cards

CardWhat it means
Memorieshow many memories are stored right now. Grows as your agents learn. A sudden drop is worth investigating, check the backup section in recipes.
Connected Agentshow many of your agents (Hermes, OpenClaw, Claude Code, …) are registered to use this brain.
Embedding Providerwhich "fingerprinting" engine is active. Local providers (like Ollama) keep everything on your machine; cloud providers may cost per use but are usually higher quality.
Access Levelsthe distribution of public / trusted / private across your memories, your privacy mix at a glance.
Memory AI (Fuel)which AI currently sorts old memories, and this month's spend against the $5 cap. The switch next to it turns memory-sorting on or off, see Fuel for when to flip it.

The action bar

ButtonWhat it does
🔎 Inspectoropens a detailed view to browse individual memories, search, read, edit, delete single entries.
📥 Backupcreates a complete backup of all memories (including private ones), written with restrictive permissions. Do this regularly; it's your undo button for anything.
❤️ Healthchecks that Qdrant, the Nexus service and configured providers respond. Green = all good. Problems show with a short reason.
🔍 Detect Agentsscans your machine for AI agents (Hermes, OpenClaw, Claude Code, Cursor, …) and registers the ones that should use Nexus.
🔄 Refreshre-reads all numbers. No side effects, safe to click anytime.
📘 Docsopens this handbook in a new tab.
Nothing here can break your data. Every button is read-only except Backup (creates a file) and single-entry edits in the Inspector. Rest easy, click around.

The graph view

The Graph shows how your memories connect: clusters = related knowledge areas; isolated dots = knowledge nothing points to (candidates for cleanup); lines = discovered relationships. It's a map, not a control panel, browse it to discover what your agents collectively know.
